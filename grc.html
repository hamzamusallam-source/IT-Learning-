IT Governance, Risk & Compliance

Showing that IT is under control: governance frameworks, risk management, security standards, data protection and audit readiness.

Path 13Level: Mid → Advanced8 topics≈ 6 weeks

Topic 1IT Governance

Governance makes sure IT investments support the organisation's goals and are well controlled.

  • Clear decision rights and committees.
  • Alignment between IT and business strategy.
  • Performance measurement.
  • Accountability at board level.

Topic 2COBIT

COBIT is a framework for governance and management of enterprise IT.

  • Governance and management objectives.
  • Design factors to tailor the system.
  • Capability and maturity levels.
  • Works alongside ITIL and ISO standards.

Topic 3Risk Management

Identifying what could go wrong, how likely it is, and what to do about it.

  • Identify, assess, treat, monitor.
  • Likelihood × impact.
  • Treat, transfer, avoid or accept.
  • A living risk register with owners.

Topic 4ISO 27001

The international standard for an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

  • Scope, context and leadership commitment.
  • Risk assessment and Statement of Applicability.
  • Annex A controls.
  • Internal audits and continual improvement.

Topic 5NCA ECC

The Essential Cybersecurity Controls from Saudi Arabia's National Cybersecurity Authority.

  • Mandatory for government and critical national infrastructure entities.
  • Main domains: governance, defence, resilience, third-party and cloud, industrial control systems.
  • Self-assessment and compliance reporting.
  • Maps well to ISO 27001 controls.

Topic 6PDPL

Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law governs how personal data is collected and used.

  • Lawful basis and clear purpose for processing.
  • Rights of data subjects.
  • Rules for transferring data outside the Kingdom.
  • Breach notification duties.

Topic 7IT Audit

Independent review that controls exist and actually work.

  • Audit planning based on risk.
  • Testing design and operating effectiveness of controls.
  • Evidence collection.
  • Findings, recommendations and follow-up.

Topic 8Policies & Procedures

Written rules that turn frameworks into daily practice.

  • Policy (what) vs procedure (how).
  • Short, clear and actually read.
  • Owners and review dates.
  • Linked to controls and audit evidence.
