IT Service Management

Running IT as a service to the business: clear processes, agreed service levels, and steady day-to-day operations.

Path 11Level: Beginner → Mid8 topics≈ 5 weeks

Topic 1ITIL 4

ITIL is the most widely used framework for IT service management.

  • The service value system and value chain.
  • Guiding principles: focus on value, start where you are, keep it simple.
  • 34 management practices.
  • Adapt it to your organisation — don't copy it word for word.

Topic 2Service Desk

The single point of contact between users and IT.

  • Multiple channels: portal, phone, email, chat.
  • Self-service and a knowledge base.
  • First-contact resolution.
  • Friendly, clear communication.

Topic 3Incident Management

Restoring normal service as quickly as possible.

  • Log, categorise and prioritise by impact and urgency.
  • Escalation paths and major incident process.
  • Keep users informed.
  • Close only when the user confirms the fix.

Topic 4Problem Management

Finding and removing the root causes of incidents.

  • Reactive: investigate after repeated incidents.
  • Proactive: find weaknesses before they hurt.
  • Known errors and workarounds.
  • Root-cause analysis techniques such as 5 Whys.

Topic 5Change Management

Making changes safely without slowing the organisation down.

  • Standard, normal and emergency changes.
  • Risk assessment and approval.
  • Change calendar and freeze periods.
  • Review failed changes to learn from them.

Topic 6SLAs

Service Level Agreements set clear expectations between IT and the business.

  • Availability and response/resolution targets.
  • OLAs between internal teams.
  • Measure and report regularly.
  • Targets the business actually cares about.

Topic 7Monitoring & NOC

The Network Operations Center watches infrastructure and services around the clock.

  • Event management: filter noise, act on what matters.
  • Runbooks for common alerts.
  • Shift handovers.
  • Link alerts to incidents automatically.

Topic 8CMDB

The Configuration Management Database records IT assets and how they depend on each other.

  • Configuration items (CIs) and relationships.
  • Know what a change or outage will affect.
  • Automated discovery to keep it accurate.
  • An outdated CMDB is worse than none.
