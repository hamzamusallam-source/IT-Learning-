Data & Analytics

Turning raw records into clear answers: collecting, cleaning, modelling and presenting data so people can make better decisions.

Path 06Level: Beginner → Mid8 topics≈ 6 weeks

Topic 1Data Fundamentals

Before analysis comes understanding what data you have and what it means.

  • Structured (tables), semi-structured (JSON) and unstructured (text, images).
  • Data types, keys and relationships.
  • Descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analytics.
  • Always ask: what decision will this analysis support?

Topic 2SQL for Analysis

SQL is the common language for asking questions of data.

  • SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY to retrieve and filter.
  • GROUP BY with SUM, COUNT, AVG to summarise.
  • JOINs to combine tables.
  • Window functions for running totals and rankings.

Topic 3Data Modelling

A good model makes data easy to query and hard to misread.

  • Facts (measurements) and dimensions (context).
  • Star schema for reporting.
  • Clear naming and one definition per metric.
  • Model for the questions people actually ask.

Topic 4ETL & Pipelines

Pipelines move data from source systems to where it is analysed.

  • Extract from sources, Transform to clean and shape, Load into the target.
  • ELT loads first and transforms inside the warehouse.
  • Schedule, monitor and alert on failures.
  • Make pipelines re-runnable without creating duplicates.

Topic 5Warehouses & Lakes

Where analytical data lives.

  • Data warehouse: structured, cleaned, optimised for reporting.
  • Data lake: raw data of any type, stored cheaply.
  • Lakehouse: combines both approaches.
  • Choose based on users, data types and cost.

Topic 6Dashboards & BI

Dashboards put the right numbers in front of the right people.

  • Start from the decision, then choose the metrics.
  • A few clear charts beat many busy ones.
  • Consistent colours, labels and time ranges.
  • Show trends and targets, not just totals.

Topic 7Data Quality

Bad data leads to confident wrong decisions.

  • Dimensions: accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness.
  • Validate data at the point it enters the system.
  • Automated checks in pipelines.
  • Fix problems at the source, not in every report.

Topic 8Data Governance

Governance decides who owns data, who can use it, and how.

  • Data owners and stewards for each domain.
  • A shared glossary of business terms.
  • Access control and data classification.
  • Compliance with data protection rules such as PDPL.
