Enterprise Architecture & Digital Transformation

Seeing the whole organisation — business, data, applications and technology — and using that picture to plan real transformation.

Path 12Level: Mid → Advanced8 topics≈ 6 weeks

Topic 1Enterprise Architecture

Enterprise architecture is the blueprint that connects business strategy to the systems that deliver it.

  • Four domains: business, data, application, technology.
  • Current state (as-is) and target state (to-be).
  • A roadmap to move between them.
  • Principles and standards to guide decisions.

Topic 2TOGAF

TOGAF is the most widely used enterprise architecture framework.

  • The Architecture Development Method (ADM) cycle.
  • Architecture repository and building blocks.
  • Stakeholder management and viewpoints.
  • Tailor it to the size of your organisation.

Topic 3Capability Mapping

A capability map shows what the organisation does, independent of how or who.

  • Stable view that survives reorganisations.
  • Assess each capability's maturity.
  • Find gaps and duplicated systems.
  • Prioritise investment where it matters most.

Topic 4Digital Strategy

A digital strategy explains how technology will help reach business goals.

  • Start from the organisation's vision and goals.
  • Maturity assessment of where you are today.
  • Prioritised initiatives with clear owners.
  • KPIs to show progress.

Topic 5Process Redesign

Digitising a bad process just makes it fail faster. Fix the process first.

  • Map the current process end to end.
  • Remove waste, delays and duplicate approvals.
  • Design around the customer's journey.
  • Then decide what to automate.

Topic 6E-Services

Delivering services online so people don't need to visit, call or print.

  • Design from the user's point of view.
  • Simple forms and clear status updates.
  • Integration between back-end systems.
  • Accessibility and Arabic/English support.

Topic 7Change Management

Technology changes are easy compared with changing how people work.

  • Explain why the change matters.
  • Involve users early.
  • Training and support during rollout.
  • Measure adoption, not just go-live.

Topic 8Vision 2030

Saudi Vision 2030 makes digital transformation a national priority.

  • Digital government and e-services.
  • Data and AI as national capabilities.
  • Cloud-first policies.
  • Building local digital skills.
