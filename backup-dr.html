Backup, DR & Business Continuity

Making sure the organisation can keep working — or recover quickly — when systems fail, data is lost or an attack hits.

Path 10Level: Mid8 topics≈ 5 weeks

Topic 1Backup Strategy

A backup strategy answers: what do we protect, how often, for how long, and where?

  • Classify systems and data by importance.
  • Full, incremental and differential backups.
  • Retention periods based on business and legal needs.
  • Automate, monitor and report on every job.

Topic 23-2-1 Rule

A simple rule that survives most disasters.

  • 3 copies of your data.
  • 2 different types of storage.
  • 1 copy off-site.
  • Modern extension: 3-2-1-1-0 — one offline or immutable copy, zero restore errors.

Topic 3RPO & RTO

Two numbers that drive every recovery design.

  • RPO (Recovery Point Objective): how much data loss is acceptable.
  • RTO (Recovery Time Objective): how long the service can be down.
  • Agree them with the business, system by system.
  • Lower RPO and RTO cost more — spend where it matters.

Topic 4Replication

Copying data continuously to another location so it's ready when needed.

  • Storage, database or application-level replication.
  • Synchronous vs asynchronous.
  • Replication copies mistakes too — it is not a backup.
  • Keep backups alongside replication.

Topic 5DR Sites

Where you recover to when the main site is lost.

  • Cold site: space and power, little else — slow to start.
  • Warm site: partly ready — hours to start.
  • Hot site: running and ready — minutes.
  • Cloud regions are now a common DR site.

Topic 6Ransomware Recovery

Ransomware targets backups first, so recovery must be planned for it.

  • Immutable or offline backup copies.
  • Separate credentials for backup systems.
  • Scan restored data before going live.
  • An isolated clean-room environment for recovery.

Topic 7Business Continuity Plan

The BCP covers people and processes, not only IT systems.

  • Business impact analysis: which processes matter most.
  • Alternate ways of working during disruption.
  • Communication plans and contact lists.
  • Clear roles and decision authority.

Topic 8DR Testing

An untested plan is just a hopeful document.

  • Tabletop exercises to walk through the plan.
  • Partial and full failover tests.
  • Measure actual RTO and RPO against targets.
  • Update the plan after every test.
