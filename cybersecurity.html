Cybersecurity

Protecting systems, identities and data — and knowing exactly what to do when something goes wrong.

Path 03Level: Beginner → Practitioner10 topics≈ 8 weeks

Topic 1Security Fundamentals

Security starts with a few ideas that apply everywhere.

  • CIA triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.
  • Threat, vulnerability and risk are different things.
  • Defence in depth: several layers, never one wall.
  • Least privilege: give only the access that is needed.

Topic 2Network Security

Controlling what can reach what, and spotting traffic that shouldn't be there.

  • Firewalls and next-generation firewalls.
  • Segmentation to limit how far an attacker can move.
  • IDS / IPS to detect and block known attacks.
  • Secure remote access with VPN or ZTNA.

Topic 3Identity & Access (IAM)

Most breaches start with a stolen or misused identity, so identity is the new perimeter.

  • Authentication (who you are) vs authorization (what you can do).
  • Multi-factor authentication for every user.
  • Privileged access management for admin accounts.
  • Joiner, mover, leaver processes to keep access clean.

Topic 4Endpoint Security

Laptops, servers and phones are where attacks usually land.

  • EDR tools that detect and respond, not just antivirus.
  • Patching and secure configuration baselines.
  • Disk encryption and device management.
  • Application control to block unknown software.

Topic 5Zero Trust

Zero Trust means never assuming trust because of network location. Every request is verified.

  • Verify explicitly: identity, device health, context.
  • Least-privilege access, granted just in time.
  • Assume breach: segment and monitor everything.
  • A journey of many small steps, not a single product.

Topic 6SOC & SIEM

The Security Operations Center watches the organisation around the clock. The SIEM is its central tool.

  • SIEM collects and correlates logs from many sources.
  • Use cases and alert rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.
  • Analyst tiers: triage, investigation, threat hunting.
  • SOAR automates repetitive response steps.

Topic 7Threat Intelligence

Knowing who is likely to attack, how, and with what — so defences focus on real threats.

  • Indicators of compromise: IPs, domains, file hashes.
  • Tactics, techniques and procedures (TTPs).
  • Strategic, operational and tactical intelligence.
  • Sharing with trusted communities and sector CERTs.

Topic 8Incident Response

A calm, rehearsed process for when an attack happens.

  • Prepare — plans, contacts, tools.
  • Detect & analyse — confirm and scope the incident.
  • Contain, eradicate, recover — stop the spread, clean up, restore.
  • Lessons learned — improve so it doesn't happen again.

Topic 9Ethical Hacking

Testing systems the way an attacker would — with permission — to find weaknesses first.

  • Phases: reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, reporting.
  • Vulnerability assessment vs penetration testing.
  • Clear scope and written authorisation, always.
  • The report and fixes matter more than the exploit.

Topic 10Security Awareness

People are both the biggest risk and the strongest defence.

  • Recognising phishing and social engineering.
  • Strong passwords and password managers.
  • Reporting suspicious activity quickly, without blame.
  • Short, regular training beats one long annual session.
