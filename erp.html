ERP & Enterprise Applications

How ERP systems model finance, supply chain and operations, how they are implemented and administered, and how they connect to everything else.

Path 08Level: Mid8 topics≈ 6 weeks

Topic 1ERP Fundamentals

Enterprise Resource Planning brings an organisation's core processes into one integrated system with one shared database.

  • Modules for finance, procurement, inventory, HR and more.
  • One source of truth instead of disconnected spreadsheets.
  • Configuration vs customisation — configure first.
  • Implementation is a business project, not only an IT one.

Topic 2Finance

The financial modules record every transaction and produce the organisation's accounts.

  • General Ledger and chart of accounts.
  • Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable.
  • Fixed Assets and Cash Management.
  • Period close and financial reporting.

Topic 3Supply Chain

Planning and moving goods from suppliers to customers.

  • Demand and supply planning.
  • Order management and fulfilment.
  • Logistics and shipping.
  • Visibility across the whole chain.

Topic 4Inventory

Knowing what stock you have, where it is, and what it's worth.

  • Items, organisations, sub-inventories and locators.
  • Receipts, issues and transfers.
  • Cycle counting and physical inventory.
  • Costing methods and valuation.

Topic 5Purchasing

The procure-to-pay process, from request to supplier payment.

  • Requisition → purchase order → receipt → invoice → payment.
  • Supplier management and approved lists.
  • Approval hierarchies and limits.
  • Three-way match: PO, receipt and invoice.

Topic 6Manufacturing

Turning materials into products, on schedule and at the right cost.

  • Bills of materials and routings.
  • Discrete and process manufacturing.
  • Work orders and batches.
  • Quality checks and cost tracking.

Topic 7ERP Administration

Keeping the ERP system healthy, secure and current.

  • User, role and responsibility management.
  • Patching and upgrades.
  • Concurrent jobs and scheduled processes.
  • Performance and environment cloning.

Topic 8Integration & APIs

ERP rarely works alone; it exchanges data with banks, e-invoicing, CRM, HR and more.

  • REST and SOAP APIs.
  • Middleware and integration platforms.
  • File-based interfaces for legacy systems.
  • Error handling and reconciliation.
