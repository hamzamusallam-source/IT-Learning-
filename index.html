Digital transformation, step by step

Technology alone does not transform an organization. These five steps show where each topic on this site fits.

  1. 1

    Assess where you are

    Map current processes, systems, data and skills. Decide which problems are worth solving first.

    Related: Networking

  2. 2

    Modernize the foundation

    Reliable networks, cloud platforms and databases make every later step possible.

    Related: Cloud, Azure

  3. 3

    Connect and clean the data

    Join ERP, operational and reporting data so decisions rest on one trusted source.

    Related: Databases, Oracle

  4. 4

    Automate and add insight

    Automate repeatable work, then apply analytics and AI where the data supports it.

    Related: Artificial Intelligence

  5. 5

    Protect and sustain

    Secure identities and data, train people and keep improving. Security runs through every step, not only the last.

    Related: Cybersecurity
