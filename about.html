About this site

Hamza IT Learning is a free, structured place to learn technology — from infrastructure and networking to cybersecurity, cloud, AI, data, business systems and IT management.

Why it exists

Most people don't struggle with technology because it's hard. They struggle because nobody showed them the order to learn it in. Every path here starts from the basics and builds step by step.

How it's written

  • Concepts before products: learn how something works first, then how each platform implements it.
  • Plain language: terms are explained the first time they appear.
  • Tied to real work: examples come from real networks, SOCs and projects.

Who writes it

Written and maintained by Hamza Musallam, in Riyadh.
